## What was dispatched, and why only these two

Both remaining candidates were verified **still broken on main by direct reading
of the code**, not by trusting the backlog entry.

**A. `H-CHECK-DUNE-COMMENT-GLUED-RULE`** (`harness/dune-comment-glued-rule`).
Two shapes, one root cause, in `check_universe_deps.sh`:

- *Leading glued comment hides a whole rule.* The awk program parses in paragraph
  mode (`RS=""`) and the rule branch reads `/^\(rule/ { block = strip_comments($0) ... }`
  — stripping happens **inside** the action, i.e. after the pattern already
  matched the raw record. dune permits a `;` comment directly above a rule with
  no blank line between; such a record starts with `;`, so `/^\(rule/` never
  matches and the rule is **invisible to the guard** — its `(universe)` status is
  never recorded.
- *Trailing comment can hijack run-target resolution.* `strip_comments()` is
  `gsub(/(^|\n)[ \t]*;[^\n]*/, "\n", b)` — **whole-line comments only**. A
  `%{dep:...}` inside a trailing same-line comment survives into the block the
  run-target regex scans.

Both are inert in the real tree today, which is exactly why fixtures are the
deliverable rather than a green build.

**B. `H-REPO-ROOT-SET-BUT-INVALID-SILENT-FALLTHROUGH`** (`harness/repo-root-set-but-invalid`).
When a caller exports `REPO_ROOT` to something failing the `[ -d ]` guard, all
three implementations (`_check_lib.sh:repo_root()`, `write_audit.sh:_repo_root()`,
`record_qc_audit.sh:_repo_root()`) fall through to the walk-up, write the record
to the **walked-up** root with `rc=0` and no diagnostic, and re-export that value
to the child. The failure this whole `H-*` family exists to prevent — a record
landing in a root the caller did not choose — is still reachable via malformed
input.

**Why these two in parallel is safe:** they touch disjoint files
(`check_universe_deps*.sh` vs `_check_lib.sh` / `write_audit.sh` /
`record_qc_audit*.sh`), and their `dev/status/harness.md` entries are ~39 lines
apart, comfortably outside git's 3-line hunk context. I deliberately rejected a
