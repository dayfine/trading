## Three operational failures this run, and one of them is mine

### A subagent printed the PAT again — second occurrence in two days

The #2675 behavioral reviewer ran
`echo "GH_TOKEN: ${GH_TOKEN:+set}${GH_TOKEN:-UNSET}"`, and the second expansion
printed the **literal token value** into its log. **`BOT_GITHUB_TOKEN` should be
