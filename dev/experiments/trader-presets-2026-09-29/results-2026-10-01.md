### Continuation buys — the dial never fired

T1's `trades.csv` equals H's at all six cells on every column except one `position_id` label
(5r s0: OMC `-2407` vs `-2408`); equity curves are byte-identical. Mechanically T1's Calmar is
"≥ H" at 3/3 and the rule would print "Adds", but a tie produced by **zero continuation
entries** is not a measurement of continuation buys. Recorded as **not tested**.

Why (code trace, #3056): continuation hits appear in the trade audit only as screener
alternatives (14 times in 5r s0, six symbols), never entered. The candidate's entry ticket is
anchored at the old base top (`breakout_price × 1.005`), not the continuation consolidation high;
with the hybrid's `sim_entry_trigger_at_suggested` + `entry_extension_max_pct 2.0` a name already
above that level gaps through the StopLimit and never fills. Mature Stage 2 also scores 0 stage
points vs 30 for a fresh breakout. Per-entry-type split (rule 3): continuation 0 trades;
base-breakout = all trades.

Consequence: **T2 vs T1 is in effect T2 vs H** — it measures the 10-week trailing stop alone.

## 5. What this says (the why)

- **The trader dials cannot be judged on the hybrid base.** The hybrid already carries the
  trader's 4 % fallback stop on ~70 % of entries; those entries are the loss engine, and every
  trader dial layered on top either never fires (continuation) or amplifies turnover into them
  (10-week trail). A coherent trader preset needs its own entry (continuation buys that actually
  fill, #3056) before the trader-vs-investor question is answerable.
- **Exit dials that shorten holding periods raise turnover, and turnover on this base is
  negative-EV.** Consistent with `project_edge_is_the_fat_tail`: the edge is in letting the few
  winners run; the T2 loss is mostly the cost of the extra entries, not the cut winners.
- **The investor preset is the robust result**: first in all six cells, both regimes (GFC and
  2021–26), salt-stable at 5d (Calmar 0.232–0.235).
- **T0 is an anomaly worth one investigation**, not a candidate: its edge is profitable trailing
  stop exits on a different set of entries, on one window only (5d unreadable).

This is a preset comparison, not a promotion (rule 6): no default flips.
