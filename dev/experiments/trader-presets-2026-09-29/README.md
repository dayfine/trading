# Trader presets vs investor vs hybrid (#3038)

**Question.** What does each *trader* dial contribute, measured as a coherent preset rather than
a one-knob graft onto the investor base? And how do the trader, hybrid and investor presets rank
over several horizons?

**Book basis.** See `docs/design/weinstein-book-reference.md` §4.6, resolved question 2026-09-29.
- The trader's buy is defined against the **30-week** MA, and the ideal trader entry is the
  continuation buy.
- The book calls the 10-week MA "best for traders to use" but never says what it is applied to.
- The 4–6 % automatic initial stop is the trader's fallback (§5.3).
- Traders exit as the Stage-3 top forms, and buy full size on the breakout.

## Reading rule (pre-registered 2026-09-29, before any cell runs)

1. **Gate.** Every paired read passes `validator_diff -check V6` at the same salt. A failing salt
   is reported and left out of that pair's reading.
2. **Metrics per salt.** Calmar (primary), max DD, total return, trades. Also the paired per-salt
   differences, arm − H and arm − I.
3. **Per-dial reading, within a window.**
   - **Continuation buys (T1 vs H).** "Adds" if T1's Calmar ≥ H's in ≥ 2 of 3 salts **and** T1's
     max DD is never > 5 pp worse. "Dilutes" if T1's Calmar is lower in ≥ 2 of 3.
     - Always report the per-entry-type split: continuation vs base-breakout trades, with count,
       realized P&L, win rate and median `pnl_percent`.
   - **10wk stage (T0 vs H).** Same rule. The book prior says T0 should not beat H; a T0 win is
     flagged for investigation, not adopted.
4. **Preset ranking, per window.** Rank I / H / T1 (/ T2) by median Calmar across valid salts, and
   report max DD alongside.
   - A ranking that flips between `5r` and `5d` is reported as regime-dependent, not resolved.
   - `26` s0 is one salt: descriptive only unless s1/s2 run.
5. **Per-trade check for any arm that "wins".** Join the arm to H on (entry date, symbol) and
   report:
   - the shared trades' `pnl_percent` deltas;
   - the unshared trades' counts and P&L;

   so a win can be attributed to shared-trade behaviour or to path (`lane-m-result.md` shape).
6. **This is a preset comparison, not a promotion.** No default flips. A promotion would need a
   ledger ACCEPT and the confirmation grid (`promotion-confirmation.md`).
