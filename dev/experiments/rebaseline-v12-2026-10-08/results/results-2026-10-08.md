**What the design can claim:**

## Why

1. **Total-return accounting is a stable ordinary stream plus a few specials.**
   - The ordinary stream is +0.142 log in every salt, about 1.05 %/yr on invested capital at 51 % invested. It is the same stream v11 measured: 0.120 + 0.018 ADR = 0.138.
   - The +0.13 estimate covered only this part.
   - Specials add +0.065 to +0.093: AD is +0.0465 everywhere, and TDG's two specials appear only where the path held TDG.
   - Interest is +0.235 (≈ 0.89 %/yr of NAV) in both arms.
   - SPY's own dividend is about 1.77 %/yr, all of it captured, while the book captures about 0.8–0.9 %/yr. So moving both sides to total return **narrows** the CAGR margin by 0.207 log in s0: from +0.373 against SPY price to +0.166 against SPY total return.
2. **The guard's effect is small and surgical.** It touches three events per path. Without it, v11 carried phantom shares worth 0.016–0.033 log and rescaled stops: WING 27.36 vs 29.32, TDG 124.48 vs 144.13, DHLGY 39.35 vs 41.32.
3. **Path is an at-fill knife-edge, and it is smaller here because the fat tail is common to all cells.**
   - Credits scale NAV and sizing scales with NAV, so cash relative to ticket size is unchanged on average.
   - The credits only re-roll which marginal ticket clears the at-fill or placement cash test: BBWI ($13,088 short), NJDCY (one more ticket) and IRM vs CVM.
   - In v12, ADMA, GLNG, MMYT and INVX are in all six cells, so the path spread fell from 0.31 to 0.14 log.
