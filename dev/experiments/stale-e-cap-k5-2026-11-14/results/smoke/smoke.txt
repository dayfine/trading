## Rework of #3608, iteration 1 (2026-11-14): the data-terms rule, the by-year table, the combined table's channel readings
# Nothing was re-run except the read: split.sh on the committed smoke artifacts above and the local data/, on the host
# (BSD awk 20200816). The first version of the read is the fe4d7ca89 commit's split.txt, which this one replaces.
#   VALIDITY=skip sh read/split.sh results/smoke <local data/> > results/smoke/split.txt
#   sh read/combine_check.sh > results/smoke/combine_check.txt

The defect. The first split.txt printed per-key lines and sums that included open positions marked from the bar store.
Each such line was a marked read of one raw close: an open key's NAV-% value, with its committed share count, entry
price and the committed NAV, gives that name's last-curve-date close to the cent (the QC review showed it for one key).
The same held for WRB, TOL and WLP1 keys, for the PS/PC sums, for the totals and for "A gate", all of which had open
keys in them.

The rule now (README §What the read prints). No printed sum includes a key whose ticket either arm holds open at the
window end ("open-leg key"); every key line is of a key with closed legs; every table says how many open-leg keys it
left out; the marks feed only the pass or fail of the bars (D, trim3, A without its 3 largest). Unmarked open-leg keys
are counted and make the outcome PROVISIONAL.

What changed in split.txt (S:C pair, 39 keys: 31 with closed legs, 8 open-leg):
  - top-key line: ELGX, MOND_old, TBI1 / IMAX, MENT, FAST (closed legs only), "(8 open-leg keys left out)";
  - the PC, PS, A, displaced-cash, total and trim3 sums, and n M0 (11 -> 9), are over the 31 keys with closed legs; all
    three of the displaced-cash tickets are open-leg keys, so that sum is empty. The first version's values of these
    sums are not repeated here: differences between old and new sums would give back the open keys' marked sums;
  - new: the open-leg table (8 keys, 8 marked, 0 unmarked; 3 PC, 3 PS, 2 M0), the by-year table (4b), and "left out"
    counts on the admission table and every component line;
  - unchanged: sections 1-3, F, L, the weights, the outcome, and every verdict-bearing RULE field (n, median, pos, neg,
    delta, Dp, Tp, mdd, L, F, Mp, Ale0, gbad), as the bars still read the marked values. RULE lines gain unmarked=0.
S:G: 5 open-leg keys (the same 5 names as S's, G holding 3,123 EOG shares), EOG's M key among them.
