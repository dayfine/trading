## Pre-registered reading, item by item

### 1. Implementation check

**How the log contributions are computed.** Each cell's daily cash path is rebuilt from the committed ledger, starting at $1,000,000 on 2000-01-01. For each calendar day d (the simulator steps every calendar day):
- **Interest:** interest_d = max(0, cash_{d−1}) × max(0, DTB3_d − 0.10) / 100 / 360, with DTB3 forward-filled from `trading/test_data/macro/tbill_3m_dtb3.csv`.
- **Dividends:** dividend_d = Σ over positions held at the prior close (entry fill < d ≤ exit fill) of shares-at-d × `unadjusted_amount`, for each row with ex_date d in `data/<A>/<Z>/<SYM>/dividends.csv`.
  - shares-at-d = `trades.csv` quantity ÷ the `splits.csv` factors dated after d, because `trades.csv` reports post-split quantity and prices. Example: CTO shows 25,035 sh at 19.49 for 8,345 sh held through the 2022-07-01 3:1 split.
- **Fills:** entry −q·entry_price − commission; exit +(q·entry_price + pnl_dollars) − commission; commission max($1, $0.01·q), from `params.sexp`.

tr1 adds the interest and dividends to cash; for tr0 they are a counterfactual on its own path. Calendar-day credits are summed onto equity-curve rows: x_t is the credit over (row_{t−1}, row_t]. Then **L_x = Σ_t ln(V_t / (V_t − x_t))**, where V_t = `portfolio_value` in `results/<cell>-equity_curve.csv`.
- L_x is the log of the actual terminal NAV over the NAV the same book would have reached had every credit been withheld, with the book scaled down proportionally: the credit plus its compounding.
- The first-order sum Σ x_t / V_t agrees to the third decimal.
- The path term is P = ln(V_T^tr1 / V_T^tr0) − L_both.
- The analyst's reconstruction was a scratch script outside the repo. qc-results re-derived the item-1 log contributions to 4 decimals with an awk port, committed as `results/analysis/tr_reconstruct.awk`. Its inputs are DTB3, a flattened dividends file and a flattened splits file built from the host store, plus `trades.csv`, `open_positions.csv` and `equity_curve.csv`. ## Why

1. **Interest = rate × a cash share the rules fix.**
   - Cash averages ~49 %. The log value of holding 100 % cash, by period:

     | period | log value of 100 % cash |
     |---|---:|
     | whole window | 0.492 (net 1.86 %/yr) |
     | 2000–02 | 0.107 |
     | 2005–07 | 0.121 |
     | 2008 | 0.013 |
     | 2009–15 | 0.001 |
     | 2016–19 | 0.049 |
     | 2020–21 | 0.003 |
     | 2022 | 0.019 |
     | 2023–25 | 0.140 |

   - 24 % of days had a net rate of zero after the 10 bp fee floor.
   - The book earned 0.073 of its 0.232 in 2000–02, when it was 9.5 % (2001) and 1.5 % (2002) invested while T-bills paid 3.55 %/yr. That is why tr1's 2000–02 era vs SPY is larger than tr0's (1.79× vs 1.71×).
   - The term is salt-invariant because the cash share is: 50.9–51.3 % invested in tr1, 50.3–50.7 % in tr0.
2. **Dividends are lumpy, and they interact with stops.**
   - **AD** (tr1 s0) end to end:
     - Bought 2025-08-04 at 72.56, 19,526 sh, stop 67.46.
     - On the $23 ex-date (2025-08-20) it opened at 51.26 (low 50.69), and the stop filled at the open for −$415,958 (−29.36 %) in `trades.csv`.
     - The same morning $449,098 was credited, so the economic result was ≈ +$33k.
     - A broker reducing resting sell stops for the dividend (FINRA Rule 5330, to be verified before encoding) would have held the stop at 44.46, which would not have triggered (#3174).
     - tr0 booked the same exit (14,021 sh, −$298,687) with no offset.
   - **BKE** has the same shape: $6 special, stop 45.76, open 40.58, adjusted stop 39.76 vs a low of 40.01.
   - **TDG**: the $22 special was also treated as a split (f = 1.1585).
     - `trades.csv` shows entry 136.70 against bars of 158.25–160.68, and 3,446 sh against ≈ 2,975 held.
     - tr0's "price-only" TDG trade reads +0.98 % instead of −12.8 %.
     - tr1 additionally credits 3,446 × $22 = $75,812.
   - **WING** (f 1.0705) and **BCH** (f 1.0719) repeat the TDG case. The dividend factor P/(P−D) snapped to a small rational (22/19, 15/14) for those three, but not for AD (1.434) or BKE (1.146).
3. **Path is an at-fill cash knife-edge, not "more cash → more trades".**
   - Credits scale NAV and sizing scales with NAV, so cash relative to ticket size is unchanged on average; the credits only re-roll which marginal tickets clear the at-fill cash test (#3138).
   - That test binds 32–38 times per run because the book runs cash to ≈ $0 on 420–500 days per cell; `min_cash_pct 0.30` is inert (`project_envelope_knobs_dead`).
   - Flips land on fat-tail trades (ADMA +300 %, BBWI +138 %, GLNG +70 %), so the path term has either sign and its spread (0.31 log) exceeds the whole dividend term.
4. **Drawdown.**
   - Over tr0's worst window (2021-06-01 → 2023-10-27): tr0 31.1 / 31.0 / 31.0 %, tr1 19.3 / 20.2 / 20.2 %, and tr1 with credits removed 24.3 / 25.1 / 25.1 %.
   - So ≈ 5 points come from credits (interest in 2022–23 on 50–67 % cash, plus BKE's special) and ≈ 6–7 points from path (GLNG).
   - tr1's own maximum moves to a different episode; with credits removed it would be 27.4 / 29.0 / 29.2 %.
5. **Sharpe:** the basis switched; on a raw basis tr1 is 0.72 vs 0.60.
