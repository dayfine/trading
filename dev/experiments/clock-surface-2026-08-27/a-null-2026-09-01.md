## Reading

- **The 26y return effect is SIGN-INCONSISTENT**: +183.5 / +232.1 / −54.7.
  Two large wins, one moderate loss, a 287pp range — cell A is a
  path-lottery cell at this horizon (consistent with
  [[project_edge_is_the_fat_tail]]: a handful of monster reshuffles swing
  hundreds of pp over 26y). The salt-0 figure must no longer be quoted as a
  robust deployment-breadth win; it is one draw from a wide distribution
  whose center is positive but whose sign is not stable at n=3.
- **maxDD improves in all three salts** (−13.1 / −1.4 / −2.5) — the clock's
  drawdown reduction is now confirmed across salts in EVERY grid cell
  (A here; B and D already had it). This is the mechanism's one
  fully-composition-and-salt-robust property.
- **Sharpe follows return** (+0.085 / +0.106 / −0.011): majority-positive,
  not consistent.
- Contrast across the grid: B = tight consistent (+7.3/+6.5/+9.6);
  D = consistent negative (−39/−395/−310); A = wide sign-inconsistent.
  Breadth AND horizon both change which regime the clock lands in.

The decision does not flip, but one leg of its stated rationale weakens:
"top-3000 (deployment) wins in all tested cells" is now, precisely: **B wins
consistently (small); A wins 2-of-3 salts (large when it wins, negative
once); D loses consistently (top-1000)**. The legs that stand un-weakened:
the default-path blast radius is measured empty (trigger-at-close tickets

everywhere tested. Anyone re-opening the value question should weigh the
mechanism as: robust risk-reducer, noisy return-lottery at 26y breadth,
consistent small return win at 5y breadth, consistent return loss at
top-1000.
