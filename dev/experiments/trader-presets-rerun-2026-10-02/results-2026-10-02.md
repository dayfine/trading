## Verdict and why

By the pre-registered rule, **T1 "adds" in both windows**. The README allows a ledger entry when a
dial reads "adds" in both windows, with promotion only via the confirmation grid. The calibrated
statement is narrower than the rule's word:

- **What it says:** the continuation anchor (#3067) is live and changes outcomes. In each window,
  the change that mattered was one ticket the old anchor could not fill. Both tickets caught a
  monster (FNSR +114 %, ADMA +300 %).
- **What it does not say:** that continuation entries are a better trade set. T1's unshared trades
  without those two lose money in 5 of 6 cells, and its shared trades lose to smaller sizing in all
  6.
- **Why it came out this way:** this is the system's known structure. The edge is the fat tail
  (`project_edge_is_the_fat_tail`), and a lever that adds tickets adds lottery draws. FNSR is
  path-robust (3 of 3 salts at 5d). ADMA is not (2 of 3 salts at 5r).
- **Forward guidance:** do not promote on this. If continuation buys are pursued, the confirmation
  grid needs a pre-2009 cell and a paired per-event read keyed on the anchor kind, which needs
  #3074. A read without that cannot tell a continuation-entry edge from a lucky ticket.
- **T2 and T0S:** no promotion. T2 is regime-dependent. T0S is a 10-week stage arm, and the book
  prior rules it out regardless of its numbers.

No default flips. Ledger: one entry for T1, recorded as **"adds" by rule, rests on one trade per
window, not promotable without the grid**.
