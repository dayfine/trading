### The pre-registered rule does not produce a reading

The rule says: *every investor cell must pass `validator_diff -check V6` against its hybrid pair; a
failing salt is reported and left out.* **All three salts fail**, on the same specimen:

```
V6 GOOG 2025-01-03 (twin positions: GOOG/GOOGL) -- only in: arm
```

The investor holds GOOG and GOOGL at once (entered 2025-01-03, both stopped out 2025-02-21). That is
one issuer held twice through two share classes. The hybrid never picks it. Left out as the rule requires, zero salts
remain, so **the rule's outcome is "no reading"** — not "promising", not "worse".

What follows is descriptive only, and marked as a deviation from the rule:

- The twin's cost is bounded: each leg lost $11,607–$11,820, so the duplicated exposure is about
  −$11.7k per salt, about **−1.2 pp** of return, and it counts *against* the investor. The investor −
  hybrid gap is +38 to +61 pp in return, and Calmar is positive for the investor and negative for the
  hybrid in every salt. The twin cannot reverse that direction; it does stop the pair from counting
  as a paired read under the rule.
- The investor is also far more stable across salts (return 29.0–29.9 %, 125–126 trades) than the
  hybrid (−31.2 → −8.0 %). Fewer entries means less exposure to the salt-seeded intraday path.

To get a reading under the rule as written: set a share-class policy for GOOG/GOOGL (#3015 — a
share-class pair, not a rename twin), rerun the three investor cells, re-check V6 (~1.5 h).

### Decomposition (the rule asks for it whatever the outcome)

Joined on `(symbol, entry_date)` between the two arms at each salt:

| salt | shared entries (hybrid P&L) | hybrid-only entries with the automatic 4 % stop | hybrid-only, structural stop | investor-only entries |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 32 (−$63,525) | 122 (**−$340,002**) | 42 (−$64,535) | 94 (+$153,483) |
| 1 | 26 (−$40,678) | 114 (**−$285,070**) | 43 (−$48,293) | 99 (+$121,802) |
| 2 | 32 (−$36,882) | 124 (**−$389,872**) | 43 (−$81,958) | 94 (+$108,071) |

"Automatic 4 % stop" = `stop_initial_distance_pct` = 0.04, the `Buffer_fallback` stop the hybrid
uses when there is no support low. The investor preset's `require_structural_stop` skips exactly
those entries. The median hybrid entry sits on that fallback (p25 = p50 = 0.04 at salt 0).

- **Why (bounded to what the diff can attribute):** the hybrid's fallback-stop entries lose
  $285k–$390k per salt (−29 to −39 pp); the investor never takes them and its own entries made
  $108k–$153k. A 4 % stop on a stock with no base is inside ordinary weekly noise — 129 of salt 0's
  164 hybrid-only trades ended on `stop_loss`, 98 of them as `gap_down`. This is the measured loss
  of one cohort, not an attribution of the whole gap to one dial: the presets differ in five dials
  at once (below), and two of them are confounded with this cohort.
- **Confounded dials.** (a) `entry_order_max_rest_weeks`: the hybrid sets 0 (tickets never
  expire), the investor keeps the default 52 weeks — an entry-side dial that can by itself change
  which names each arm enters, so the investor-only cohort is not caused by
  `require_structural_stop` alone. (b) `catastrophic_stop_pct 0.10` (hybrid only): its exits land in
  `stop_loss` in `trades.csv`, and the committed artifacts do not tag them separately, so the
  decomposition the rule asked for cannot be reported for this dial from these files.
- Stage-3 / extension exits barely register at 5y: the hybrid recorded 1 `extension_stop` exit per
  salt and no `stage3_force_exit` exit, so "hold through Stage 3" had almost nothing to act on here.
- This agrees with the book's stop rule ("investors should never use automatic percentages",
  Ch. 6, cited in `QUEUE.md`) and with the 09-25 finding that tighter stops lose
  (`project_26y_review_pack_2026_09_24`). It is one 5y window on one breadth tier: no default
  changes, and a promotion case would need the confirmation grid.

**Forward guidance.** The lever to test next is `require_structural_stop` **alone** on the 26y
hybrid (queue item 5): a single-delta run isolates it from the rest-weeks and catastrophic-stop
dials above, and has no share-class twin in it.
