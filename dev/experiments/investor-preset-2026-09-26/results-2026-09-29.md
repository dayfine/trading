## What the flag did (control check)

- **Hybrid: no change at any salt.** Every `inv5sc-hybrid` metric equals `inv5-hybrid` to the
  printed digit (e.g. s0 −25.89 %, 196 trades, realized −$468,062, both builds). The hybrid never
  bought a second share class, so the rule never fired, and the build change (f5507ad86 → 557e61468)
  did not move the default path on this window.
- **Investor s0 / s2: +1.5 / +0.2 pp** (29.05 → 30.56 %, 29.90 → 30.09 %). The rule dropped the
  second GOOG/GOOGL leg that failed V6 on 09-28.
- **Investor s1: −19.0 pp** (29.95 → 10.98 %). One skipped entry rerouted the path:
  - The first differing entry is FWONK 2023-03-27. It is in the flag-off run and absent from the
    flag-on run, because FWONA's resting entry ticket (filled 2023-04-03) already counted for the
    issuer.
  - The paths then split 8 months later. Flag-off bought **ADMA 2023-12-19, +$271,518**, its largest
    winner (27 % of starting capital). Flag-on bought BMA 2023-12-18 instead (+$58,311).
  - On the 111 entries both runs share (same date and symbol), the realized-P&L difference is
    +$17,243. The drop is the ADMA path, not the rule's per-trade cost.

  Sources: `results/inv5-investor-s1-v11-trades.csv` vs `results/inv5sc-investor-s1-v11-trades.csv`.

So the s1 investor figure moved 19 pp on a single one-trade path change. At n = 3 salts the
**level** of the investor number is a path lottery. The **ranking** (investor ≫ hybrid on
Calmar and drawdown at every salt, both with and without the flag) is what survives.

## Finding: V6 did not see a second share-class pair

The flag-off s1 investor run held **FWONK (from 2023-03-27) and FWONA (from 2023-04-03) together
until 2023-06-05**. That is the same doubled-issuer exposure as GOOG/GOOGL, but
`inv5-investor-s1-v11-validator.sexp.md` flags only the GOOG pair. V6's twin detection does not
cover every share-class group in `trading/test_data/share_classes.sexp`. Filed as #3035.
It does not affect this reading: with the flag on, no cell holds two classes of one issuer, so
V6 = 0 on both arms is correct.

## Why investor ≫ hybrid (unchanged from 09-28 §2)

The 09-28 decomposition still stands. The hybrid's entries on the 4 % `Buffer_fallback` stop (no
structural stop) lose $285k–$390k per salt, and the investor preset's `require_structural_stop`
refuses exactly those entries. Two other settings also differ between the presets and are not
separated by this design: `entry_order_max_rest_weeks` (0 vs 52) and `catastrophic_stop_pct` 0.10.
Queue item 5 (`require_structural_stop` alone on the 26y hybrid) isolates the first.

## Limits

- n = 3 salts. The s1 reroute shows a single trade can move a salt by ~20 pp.
- Specs differ from the pre-registered pair by the share-class flag (stated above).
