## Decomposition vs the hybrid (trade level)

All figures come from `results/inv26sc-investor-s0-v11-trades.csv` vs
`../obvious-fixes-2026-09-25/results/f2-fills-faithful-s0-v11-trades.csv`.

### 1. Initial stop width: the largest visible difference (not isolated)

| initial stop distance | investor n | investor P&L | win | hybrid n | hybrid P&L | win |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| < 4.5 % | 48 | +$1,278k | 29 % | **495** | +$924k | 28 % |
| 4.5–8 % | 64 | −$1,037k | 30 % | 70 | +$23k | 26 % |
| 8–12 % | 164 | +$1,955k | 40 % | 56 | −$255k | 34 % |
| 12–16 % | 230 | +$2,808k | 50 % | 103 | +$529k | 47 % |

- **Hybrid.** 68 % of its entries (495 / 724) sit on a stop under 4.5 %: 428 at exactly 4.0 % (the
  `Buffer_fallback` stop, used when there is no support low) and 67 at 4.1–4.4 %.
- **Investor.** It refuses those entries (`require_structural_stop`). 78 % of its entries carry an
  8–16 % structural stop.
- **Win rate rises with stop width in the investor arm** (29 → 30 → 40 → 50 %). The hybrid is not monotone (28 → 26 → 34 → 47 %).
- **Not isolated.** The investor spec also differs from f2 on other settings (see `specs/inv26sc-investor.sexp` vs `../obvious-fixes-2026-09-25/specs/f2-fills-faithful.sexp`), so this table describes the two books; it does not attribute the gap to stop width. Queue item 5 (`require_structural_stop` alone on the hybrid) is the test that isolates it.

### 2. Exits: fewer, deeper stop-outs; winners leave by laggard rotation

| exit | investor n | P&L | median pnl % | win | hybrid n | P&L | median pnl % | win |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| stop_loss | 246 | −$7.51M | −9.0 | 11 % | 478 | −$5.47M | −4.6 | 7 % |
| laggard_rotation | 254 | +$12.18M | +4.3 (mean +14.9, p90 +44.3) | 71 % | 234 | +$6.01M | +4.8 (mean +11.1, p90 +33.6) | 77 % |
| other (delisted, liquidity; hybrid adds extension, stage-3) | 6 | +$0.33M | | | 12 | +$0.68M | | |

- **Stop-outs.** The hybrid is stopped out on 66 % of its trades, at a median −4.6 %: the tight
  fallback stops cut early and often. The investor is stopped out on 49 %, at −9.0 %.
- **Winners.** Laggard rotation (Ch. 4, "move the proceeds into a new Stage 2 stock") is the exit
  for nearly all winners in both arms. Its right tail is fatter for the investor (p90 +44 % vs
  +34 %).
- **Dollars.** Dollar P&L scales with equity, and the investor's equity is larger for most of the
  window. Compare the percentages, not the dollars.
