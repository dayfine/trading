## Implication

Phase A answers "does it trade": yes, once the non-book slow-grind gate is off and tickets are priced at the
close. It does not answer "does it make money": on this window — which contains the best short regime of the 26
years — a short-only book on these rules lost 8–10 %. Phase B (#3131, the investor-style entry at the breakdown)
and Phase C (26y, 3 salts, M1–M5) remain the user's call.
