## Why

The NAV gap splits into realised P&L and the value of positions open at the end (`equity_curve.csv` last row
minus the `trades.csv` P&L sums):

| window | salt | NAV msB − ms0 | realised Δ | open-position Δ |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 5d | 0 / 1 / 2 | −$14.7k / −$16.8k / −$16.5k | +$24.0k / +$22.0k / +$22.2k | −$38.7k each |
| 5r | 0 / 1 / 2 | −$70.0k / −$7.3k / −$55.6k | −$34.3k / −$7.7k / −$20.1k | −$35.7k / +$0.4k / −$35.5k |

The realised Δ splits into four cohorts (`pair_join.awk` + `bearish_week_fills.awk`; signs are the effect on
msB, so a removed loser counts +):

| window | salt | Bearish-week fills removed | other null-only trades removed | msB-only entries | shared trades Δ | realised Δ |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5d | 0 | +$27.3k | +$22.3k (SVVS, FUN) | −$25.3k | −$0.2k | +$24.0k |
| 5d | 1 | +$27.5k | +$21.5k (SVVS, FUN) | −$26.9k | −$0.1k | +$22.0k |
| 5d | 2 | +$26.9k | +$21.5k (SVVS, FUN) | −$26.1k | −$0.1k | +$22.2k |
| 5r | 0 | −$32.9k | +$0.4k (BRO) | −$2.2k | +$0.4k | −$34.3k |
| 5r | 1 | −$30.8k | +$13.7k (SARO) | +$10.6k | −$1.3k | −$7.7k |
| 5r | 2 | −$32.6k | $0 | +$11.9k | +$0.5k | −$20.1k |

The "slot shuffle" — msB-only entries net of the non-Bearish null trades they displaced — nets −$3.0k / −$5.4k /
−$4.6k at 5d and −$1.8k / +$24.4k / +$11.9k at 5r: small and of either sign.

Three effects, each traced:

1. **Removing bear-week fills is a coin-flip on these windows, not a clean save.** At 5d the six closed
   Bearish-week fills netted −$27.3k (s0; five losers, KOF +$8.0k) — the case the book has in mind. At 5r the
   three netted +$32.9k: AIT (+$19.9k, Oct 2022) and STN (+$22.2k, May 2025) were bought inside Bearish stretches
   that ended within weeks (AIT near the October 2022 low; the gate read Bearish to 2022-11-04), i.e. at the start
   of the recoveries. Bearish-week fills cluster at turns, and the gate is a lagging read of the turn. This is
   the main realised driver at 5r.
2. **Re-issued tickets fill late at a stale level — visible per trade, small in aggregate.** Suspension
   re-issues the ticket *unchanged*, so a ticket placed before the bear fills whenever price next reaches the old
   trigger: DLX after 7 months, STN after 11, both stopped within days (decision dates from the sweep-output
   `trade_audit.sexp`, not committed — 21 MB). Net of the trades they displaced, the re-shuffled slots cost
   only −$3k to −$5k at 5d and are mixed at 5r (table above), so this is a mechanism defect, not the source of
   the gap. It is the same far-anchor shape as `project_recovery_reentry_gap` and the short-side RMD/GFI
   tickets in `../shorts-liveness-2026-10-03/`: a resting level outlives the base that justified it.
3. **Window-end truncation (5d).** ONXX, WST and DUK filled 2012-05-24 / 06-01 in Bearish weeks in the null
   and were up $38.7k at the 2012-06-28 mark; msB suspended them and the window ended before the gate
   reopened. This term is an artefact of where the window stops, not a property of the knob — but without it
   5d would read "adds" on realised P&L (+$22k–$24k) with a better drawdown, so the 5d verdict rests on it.
   At 5r the matching open-position term (s0/s2) is path divergence: MPC, held by the null from 2026-03-02, is
   a candidate in msB but is never entered there.

**Fit with the record.** Agrees with `project_recovery_reentry_gap` (far anchors) and with the 10-02 note that
bear-week fills are 8 % of the null's entries: the knob acts on a small set, and its sign is set by a handful
of turn-of-market trades. It does not contradict the one-salt +4.6 pp on the hybrid preset (different preset,
different trade set).

**Forward.** The knob's sign is set by a few turn-of-market fills and by where the window ends, not by a
systematic saving, so it is not worth a 26y phase on this preset. A suspend that re-issues at the old level
also inherits the stale-anchor defect; if this knob is ever
revisited, it should re-qualify the ticket against the current base on re-issue (cancel and re-screen) rather
than resume it. That is a different mechanism and needs its own pre-registration; not proposed now.

## Second read (results-analyst, 2026-10-04 evening) — corrections

An independent read (`results-analyst`, opus) recomputed every figure from `results/` plus the sweep-output
`trade_audit.sexp` files. It **confirms the verdict** (rules 1–8 as above: "dilutes" both windows, no phase 2,
default `Off`) and **corrects the explanation** in five places. Where this section and the *Why* above disagree,
this section stands.

1. **The 5d result is one trade, not a window-end artefact.** ONXX (ticket decided 2012-05-04, Bullish tape;
   ms0 filled it 2012-05-24 as a genuine breakout, open 44.73 / high 46.42) was marked at 63.97 on 2012-06-28,
   +$31.8k — 1.9–2.2× the 5d equity gap. The gate stayed Bearish until 2012-07-27, and ONXX never traded below
   63.50 after June 2012, so msB's suspended ticket could never have filled: the window end hid no pending msB
   benefit. The "realised P&L would read adds" counterfactual above is asymmetric (it keeps msB's avoided closed
   losers and drops ms0's open winners). The symmetric test — truncate both NAVs — gives msB Calmar ≥ ms0 at
   3/3 salts at every cut through 2012-06-15 and at 0/3 from 2012-06-22. Correct statement: **5d is decided by
   ONXX in the final fortnight and is uninformative about the knob's sign.** Pooled, the nine 5d Bearish-week
   fills net +$11.4k for ms0.
2. **Timing at turns holds; adverse quality does not.** Bearish-week fills come a median 3 weeks before the
   screen reopens (26y descriptive read, earlier build: vs 9 weeks for a random Bearish week; SPY's next 13
   weeks +3.7–3.8 % vs +0.7 %). But they are *ordinary* fills: the 12 in both windows at s0 have mean +4.2 %,
   median +0.3 %, 6/12 winners; at 26y Bearish-week vs Bullish-week fills have the same median (≈ −2.9 %) and
   similar means. Their dollar sum is a tail lottery: +$44.2k for ms0 across 5d + 5r, −$29.7k without ONXX,
   STN and AIT. So the knob removes ordinary fills near turns — negative expectation on a fat-tailed book,
   realised size set by whether a tail winner is in the sample.
3. **MPC is a traced cash chain, not unexplained divergence.** STN's suspension left msB with ~$173k cash on
   2025-08-22 (ms0 ~$68k, holding STN), so msB placed ATMU (+$15.0k). On 2026-02-27 msB's audit skips MPC with
   `Insufficient_cash` because ATMU was still held; ms0 bought MPC (+$29.3k open at the end). At s1 the same ATMU
   holding instead crowded out SARO (−$13.7k in ms0). One Bearish-week fill's knock-on is −$26.4k / +$28.8k /
   −$12.6k by salt — why the s0/s2 Calmar gaps are 6–7× the s1 gap.
4. **The re-issue defect is a dip-buy, not a far anchor.** 3 of 4 re-issued fills filled *from above*: STN
   (bar opened 99.75 vs 88.86 trigger, fell to 87.10), ISEE (21.01 vs 19.83), CHKP (26.60 vs 25.66), with stale
   stops (V12: STN 15.1 %, ISEE 15.8 %). Every original ms0 Bearish-week fill was a breakout from below. This is
   the opposite of `project_recovery_reentry_gap`'s shape (tickets above the market that never fill) and a
   spine-item-3 faithfulness defect → issue #3126.
5. **Forward guidance, narrowed.** The evidence supports: removing ordinary fills has negative expectation here;
   a 5y × 3-salt Calmar test cannot measure levers that move a handful of fills (each window is about one draw:
   the Bearish-week fill set is identical at every salt). It does **not** support a cancel-and-re-screen arm as
   an improvement (it shares the dominant term; its maximum gain over msB is the re-issued term, −$4k to −$6k
   per window per salt) — fixing re-issue is a correctness item for the axis (#3126), not a lever. Nor does it
   support the broad "avoid levers that move/time a resting entry level". Classification: REJECT-as-default,
   keep as a book-faithful axis.

**Calibration limit.** "3 of 3 salts" replicates only the downstream cash path: the same 9 (5d) and 3 (5r)
Bearish-week fills appear at every salt. Future pre-registrations for few-fill levers need a paired per-event
estimand or the 26y window, plus a truncation-robustness check.
