# Universe depth at live size, rerun on the repaired pit-v13 lists (pre-registration)

This file is committed with the run kit, before any cell of this rerun runs. Nothing below changes after the first
result.

## Why a rerun

The first run (`../universe-depth-2026-11-23/`, `RESULTS.md`, #3721) was **INVALID** on every read. V6 failed on all
five seeds of every pair, because rename twins (one company under two tickers, e.g. GLPG/LKFT, CMD/CMN, KONA/KONAQ,
NCS/CNR_old2) were in the v13 lists, and some cells held one instrument as two positions.

PR #3727 (96b4eb2b6, merged 2026-11-26) repaired the lists: it removed 2,025 twin rows from both pit-v13 list families
(`top-3000-YYYY` and `top-4000-YYYY`) and extended `trading/devtools/checks/pit_twin_cooccur_check.sh` so a twin pair
recorded by an earlier vintage fails the check when both legs are back in a same-year list.

That repair broke the nesting of the two families: in 70 name-years (28 companies), `top-4000-Y` kept a twin leg
other than the one `top-3000-Y` kept, so top-3000 was no longer inside top-4000 (QC on this PR). PR #3734 (0890f9eb9,
merged 2026-11-27) re-nested them: `top-4000-Y` now keeps the `top-3000-Y` leg. It also added a nesting check to
`pit_twin_cooccur_check.sh`. This rerun uses #3734's lists.

The user chose, on 2026-11-26, to **rerun S3 and U4 only**.

## What is dropped, and why

- **N3 and UN4 are not rerun** (user decision, 2026-11-26). The first run's RESULTS.md §2 and §5 give the reason:
  `require_structural_stop false` is not promotable whatever the data shows (Weinstein spine item 5: the initial
  stop sits below the base), and PREREG-ADDENDUM A4 licenses nothing on a HARM or INCONCLUSIVE read. So no N3 or UN4
  result could change a decision. Their HARM shapes stay on record as shapes, not verdicts.
- With them go the N3 vs S3 and UN4 vs U4 reads, the N3 liveness rule and the parts of measures 5 and 6 that only
  concern the N arms. Nothing else changes.

## Rules carried over word for word

The rules below are quoted from `../universe-depth-2026-11-23/PREREG.md` (237b2bb8d228, #3707) and
`../universe-depth-2026-11-23/PREREG-ADDENDUM.md` (376b2f5ab059, #3719) without change. Where a quoted rule names an
arm this rerun does not run, that part has nothing to apply to; no threshold, metric or clause is added or changed.

### Tripwire and validity (PREREG.md)

> - **Build tripwire:** NAV-scale P-100k k0 (v12 warehouse `v12wk-9236`) is re-run at the run build and must reproduce
>   its `trades.csv` md5 (`results/k0/ns-P-100k-k0-s0-trades.md5.txt`). If it does not, stop.
> - **V6** on every paired read (U4 vs S3, N3 vs S3, UN4 vs U4) at the same seed. A V6-failing seed is dropped; < 4
>   valid seeds = INVALID.
> - **Liveness:**
>   - U4 is live if, on ≥ 3 of 5 seeds, at least 10 of its round trips are in names that are in top-4000 but not
>     top-3000 for that decision year.
>   - N3 is live if, on ≥ 3 of 5 seeds, at least 20 % of its entries carry a `Buffer_fallback` initial stop.
>   - A dead arm gets no read.

In this rerun the only paired read is U4 vs S3, and the only liveness rule that applies is U4's.

### What is measured (PREREG.md)

> per arm, median over seeds; whole run and 2000–08 / 2009–16 / 2017–26
>
> 1. ln(final / initial).
> 2. Max drawdown and Ulcer index.
> 3. Share of entries at or below $14 raw, and funded doublers (≥ +100 %) among them; all doublers.
> 4. Round trips by universe band (ranks 1–3000 vs 3001–4000 of that year's list) and their summed NAV%.
> 5. Entries by initial-stop kind (`Support_floor` vs `Buffer_fallback`) and the win rate, median return and doublers of each.
> 6. `No_structural_stop` skips per year (S3, U4).
> 7. Descriptive, no decision: S3 against NAV-scale P-100k (same config on v12), i.e. what the v13 universe rebuild
>    (survivorship fill + true-dollar ranking) moves on its own.

### Decision rule (PREREG.md)

> Paired Δ = ln(arm_final / control_final) at the same seed.
>
> | verdict | condition |
> |---|---|
> | PASS | median Δ ≥ +0.20, ≥ 4/5 positive, median ΔDD ≤ +3 pp |
> | PAYS-WITH-RISK | median Δ ≥ +0.20, ≥ 4/5 positive, median ΔDD > +3 pp |
> | HARM | median Δ ≤ −0.20, ≥ 4/5 negative |
> | INCONCLUSIVE | anything else |
> | DEAD / INVALID | liveness fails / < 4 valid seeds |
>
> - **U4 vs S3 (universe depth).** PASS or PAYS-WITH-RISK: depth adds return at live size, and the next step is a
>   confirmation cell (different period or vintage) before the live picks move to top-4000. INCONCLUSIVE: depth adds
>   nothing measurable; picks stay on top-3000. HARM: depth costs; do not widen.
>
> A Δ inside the 0.45-log single-cell floor that is not traced to the lever's own trades is reported as no effect.

The N3 vs S3 and UN4 vs U4 bullets of that section are not quoted because those reads are not run.

### Addendum A1–A3 (PREREG-ADDENDUM.md)

They apply to U4 vs S3 exactly as written. Quoted in full:

> **A1. A U4 PASS must be traced to the deep band**
>
> `PREREG.md` sets PASS at median Δ ≥ +0.20 with at least 4/5 seeds positive. It also says that a Δ inside the 0.45-log
> single-cell floor that is not traced to the lever's trades counts as no effect. For U4 vs S3, both rules apply, as
> follows.
>
> - **Trace test.** U4 is traced when, on at least 3 of the seeds where Δ > 0, the round trips in ranks 3001–4000
>   (measure 4) add up to at least half of that seed's Δ, both measured in NAV % terms.
>   - Knock-on effects, such as capital freed for or taken from ranks 1–3000, are reported, but they cannot carry a
>     PASS on their own.
> - **Walk.** The analyst walks the 5 largest deep-band contributors by name against the bars, because the band's
>   sampled hard-defect rate is 2.4 %.
> - **Verdict.** If the threshold is met but the trace test fails, U4 is INCONCLUSIVE.
> - **Scope.** The 0.45 floor applies to single seeds. The median over five seeds may fall below 0.45 and still PASS,
>   provided the trace test holds.
>
> **A2. A defect in the top 5 caps the verdict**
>
> If the walk confirms a bar defect in any of the top-5 deep-band contributors, U4 is capped at INCONCLUSIVE. Examples
> are a store-vs-warehouse disagreement like #3705, or a vendor splice.
>
> - **Quarantine.** The defective names are quarantined, as DNGFF, HLAN, MDFI and SYNI were.
> - **Rerun.** The U4 and S3 pair reruns on the affected seeds only, with the same specs, the same thresholds and this
>   file's rules. The rerun's paired Δ replaces the affected seeds.
> - **Why.** Subtracting the trades from Δ is not allowed: removing one name changes the whole capital path.
>
> **A3. Confirmation cell for a U4 PASS or PAYS-WITH-RISK**
>
> The live picks do not move to top-4000 until this confirmation passes. It runs only after a U4 PASS or PAYS-WITH-RISK
> (including one reached after an A2 rerun).
>
> - **Pairs.** U4 vs S3 on the v13 lists, `initial_cash` 100000 with the live-size overrides, and the same
>   `require_structural_stop` as the main run.
> - **Two fresh starts,** both running to the same end date as the main run, with K=5 new seeds each (k5–k9):
>   - 2009-03-02, after the GFC;
>   - 2017-01-03, late cycle and the 2020s.
> - **Thresholds.** Each start is judged on its own with the `PREREG.md` thresholds and A1 above. The confirmation
>   passes only if **both** starts PASS or PAYS-WITH-RISK.
> - **Why these starts.** A start after 2000 overlaps the main run's history. A fresh $100k start resets compounding and
>   position sizes, so the capital path is new even though the market is not. Two starts in different regimes guard
>   against the post-GFC small-cap bull carrying the result alone.

A4 concerns N3 only and has nothing to apply to.

## Known confounds, stated in advance

These are the first run's, minus the N-arm ones, with the first corrected and one added:

- **The v13 lists differ from v12** in three ways at once: survivorship fill, true-dollar ranking, and the #3727 twin
  repair re-nested by #3734. Both arms' lists are built by the same rules, and after #3734 every `top-3000-Y` is
  inside its `top-4000-Y`, so U4's lists are S3's plus that year's ranks 3001–4000. The changes therefore do not
  differ between the arms, but they do not cancel in the paired Δ either: they set which names are in the band and
  re-draw both arms' paths, so the Δ is the effect of depth on these lists, not on v12-style lists. Measure 7 shows
  the combined size of the changes on S3. (The first version of this file said "they cancel in the paired Δ"; on
  #3727's un-nested lists that was false, and on #3734's lists it overstates what pairing removes.)
- **Dollar-volume mis-scaling at the band edges** (#3735, `dev/experiments/adv-scaling-extent-2026-11-27/README.md`
  on its branch `experiment/adv-scaling-extent`; issue #3736). The list builder decides each split's volume
  restatement on its own, so a file whose volume is restated across some splits and not others gets a dollar volume
  off by a split factor (PPHM 2004 is 7× high). #3735 measures how many v13 cells depend on that decision. For the
  3001–4000 band, the U4-vs-S3 estimand, its figures (its R8b) are:
  - under a consistent per-file vote, at most 1.44 % of band cells over all years and 2.48 % in the worst year
    (2014), or about 1.09 % overall and 1.84 % at most (2009) without names that cross both edges;
  - at the all-restated corner, about 1.98 % overall and 3.65 % in the worst year (2016).

  Both arms are built by the same rule, so it is not a between-arm confound. It shifts which ~1–2 % of the band's
  names define "ranks 3001–4000". The A1 walk of the top-5 deep-band contributors and A2 cover a mis-scaled name
  that carries the result. The decision rule is unchanged.
- **Live-size floors** ($100k entry, $50k hold) bind more in ranks 3001–4000. That is the live configuration and is
  intended.
- **One market history**; ≥ 4/5 is a consistency check, not a significance test.
- **Data quality:** ranks 3001–4000 sampled at a 2.4 % hard-defect rate against 0.7 % in 1–3000; a big U4 effect must
  be traced to named trades and checked against the bars before it is believed (A1, A2).
- **Power, stated now from the first run's read (RESULTS.md §2):** with a per-pair SD near 0.29 log, K=5 gives the
  PASS shape about 6 % of the time at a true effect of 0, 43 % at +0.20, 68 % at +0.30 and 87 % at +0.40. An
  INCONCLUSIVE is the likely outcome unless the true effect is about +0.3 log or larger. This is context for the
  reader, not a change to the rule.
