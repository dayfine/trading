## Why — the part that transfers

1. **The lever is global; the problem is post-crash.** A 4/13/26-week high is hit by minor rallies in every
   market. 5d, entries before 2009-01-01: null 15 entries for −$32k at every salt; ia4 24 for −$138k to
   −$141k; ia13 23 for −$138k to −$141k; ia26 20 for −$107k to −$110k. In June–July 2007, with the macro
   gate still Bullish, ia4 s0 lost −$84k on the extra entries. 5r: ia4 194 trades vs 126 and DD 41.6 vs
   22.4. The recovery entries the arms did make (15–21 per salt) netted −$11k to +$19k. **The cost is paid
   in every regime; the benefit, where it exists, is a rounding error.**

2. **On the investor preset the 2009 gap is a `No_structural_stop` skip, not an unfilled ticket.** T1 (the
   hybrid, PR #3081) placed 116 tickets in May–Aug 2009 and filled none (median 60 % above the close).
   The investor null placed **2** (`grep -c '(entry_date 2009-0[5-8]' …ia0-5d-s0-v11-trade_audit.sexp`).
   The cascade admitted the top 20 every week (`cascade_summaries`, `long_top_n_admitted 20`); then
   `require_structural_stop` skipped them: with the ticket at the pre-crash high, the support-floor scan
   below it finds no correction low within 15 %, the stop falls back to the 4 % buffer, and the investor
   rule declines a fallback stop (`weinstein_strategy_config.mli`). The arms moved the ticket down, the
   floor came within range, 15–21 tickets per salt were placed and filled. Same root cause as T1 (the
   anchor), a different proximate reason; the fix is not the same for the two presets.

3. **Even when filled, the 2009 recovery entries whipsawed.** ia4 s0, 25 entries May–Sep 2009: 11 stopped
   out at −14 to −16 % within 4–50 days during the June–July 2009 pullback (13–15 % structural stops:
   THRM −14.0 % in 4 days, ALV −15.2 % in 9, CASC1 −16.4 % in 10, PXP −14.9 % in 11); the winners (EBAY
   +31.7 %, AWI +31.0 %, LPS +18.3 %) left by laggard rotation. Net about zero. Post-crash volatility
   defeats the base-low stop even when the ticket is reachable. **Any ticket-level fix has this cap.**

4. **"Cash through the recovery" is several patterns, not one** (26y investor s0, every macro reopen after
   ≥ 8 non-Bullish weeks; `results/episodes.awk`; now a pack signal, PR #3086):

   | reopen | entries 13 w / 26 w | strategy 26 w | SPY 26 w | read |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 2003-05-02 | 7 / 15 | +21.6 % | +13.8 % | caught |
   | 2009-05-08 | 0 / 10 | −1.5 % | +16.4 % | sat out |
   | 2010-10-08 | 4 / 19 | +7.4 % | +15.1 % | lagged |
   | 2012-08-10 | 8 / 14 | +20.5 % | +9.1 % | caught |
   | 2019-02-15 | 2 / 9 | −0.5 % | +5.1 % | sat out (SPY under the 8 % flag) |
   | 2020-06-05 | 6 / 15 | +25.2 % | +16.8 % | caught |
   | 2022-11-25 | 0 / 3 | −1.3 % | +5.3 % | sat out (same) |
   | 2025-05-23 | 12 / 19 | −8.0 % | +14.5 % | invested and lagged |

   Caught when the old highs had aged out of the 60-week window (2003, after a 2.5-year bear) or were
   reclaimed fast (2020); sat out when stocks were still far below their 60-week highs at reopen (2009,
   2019, 2022); and 2025 was fully invested and lost (−$379k in 26 weeks).

5. **A base-bounded anchor would be global too — do not build it** (`anchor-position-screen.md` §1).
   Among the 7,231 structural-stop-skipped candidates over 26 years, the share sitting ≥ 20 % under their
   graded top is 9 % in the calmest year (2017) and 44–47 % in 2009 and 2000; about half of all such
   skips are within 10 % of the top (skipped for want of a correction low, not for a far ticket). Far
   anchors are not post-crash-specific, so any rule that moves the anchor moves entries in every regime.

6. **2025 is picks, not cash and not path** (`anchor-position-screen.md` §2): 21 of 22 post-reopen entries
   are identical in all three salts; the picks' own 26-week forward return has median +4.7 % (10 of 22
   negative) against SPY +14.5 %; the stops were right more often than wrong. Same shape as 2018–23
   (`project_melt_up_lag_anatomy`). Entry timing is not the lever there either.

7. **Narrow structural stops are not a lever**: initial stop < 6 % over 26 years nets +$598k (s0) / +$562k
   (s1) on 67–69 trades.

**Forward guidance.** Ticket-level entry timing for the recovery is exhausted: an N-week anchor dilutes,
a base-bounded anchor is global, and the faithful rule that sits out ("prefer other candidates" when the
structural stop needs more than ~15 %) is doing what the book says. What remains needs a user decision:
`entry_ticket_macro_suspend` (free, small expected effect, cuts the bear-rally fills that the arms
suffered in 2007–08), a shorts liveness pair (prior record negative), or a selection-side screen for the
2025 shape. The SPY sleeve stays user-declined.
