**Grades (mechanical rubric, post-exit path, basis-free):** A 162 / B 209 / C 187 /
D 141 / F 15 / X 2. A = +$9.05M; B −$0.56M; C −$2.88M; D −$2.61M; F −$0.30M;

**Signal study (26-wk RS vs GSPC at Jan 1 and Jun 30, PIT universe per year, 322
sector-years, winsorized [−95%, +300%], 0.6% artifacts dropped):** Spearman(RS,
year return) −0.007 (50% positive); mid-year +0.008. Decile medians 5.9% (strongest)
… 11% (middle) … 3.8% (weakest); strongest decile has the fattest right tail
(winsorized mean +15–17% vs 13%). Of top-5-by-return names per sector-year, 11%
were top-5 by signal. Signal-best sector = market-best sector in 1/27 years.

**Catch:** record traded 17 of 1,610 top-5-by-return sector-year names (1.1%);
14% of its 658 traded name-years were top-20 in sector (base ≈ 11%). Its picks
returned +32% for the year vs +14% sector median (69% above) — inflated by
entering after the move.

**Read:** the edge is being IN the 4–8 names/yr that run 50–140% and STAYING in;
ranking does not find them and exits do not create them. Levers with measured
headroom: the 4% fallback stop (whipsaw tax > the run's net) and the funnel (1% of
winners admitted). See [[project_monster_funnel_top_of_funnel]],
[[project_fallback_stop_half_book_band]], [[project_rs_trend_dead_and_rs_value_unpredictive]],
[[project_edge_is_the_fat_tail]], [[project_exit_stack_survives_fixed_basis]].
Caveat: the signal proxy is not the screener's weekly cascade score.
