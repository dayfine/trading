Read 2026-10-25, dispatcher-side, record N arm s0 (`.sweep-output/review-breadth-N-v2`), salts ~identical post-2019.

- 2025: strat −13.4 % vs SPY TR +17.7 % (−31 pp, worst of 27 years). Median top-3000 stock +5.4 % (survivors, prior-year PIT list), so ~−12 pp is a narrow tape (not unique: 2020 −13.7, 2023 −14.6, 2024 −19.9) and ~−19 pp is our own shortfall vs the median stock (2nd worst after 2009 −31.5).
- Phases vs SPY: Jan–Feb 19 −10 pp (new buys stopped, 78 % invested); correction to Apr 8 +11 pp; Apr 8–Jun 30 rebound −24 pp (22 % invested, captured 5 % of SPY's +24.8 %; same as 2003 4 %, 2009 0 %, 2018-19 −4 %; only 2020 38 % / 2022-23 31 %); Jul–Oct −10 pp; Nov–Dec −2 pp.
- Tail drought: best trade by entry year ≥ +23 % in every non-bear year 2000–2023; 2024 entries best +12 % (WPM) in every pack, 2025 +24 % (s0; +45–66 % in tr1/v3266). 2024's +50 % came from 2023 entries (ADMA +300 %, MMYT +163 %) held over, which masked the stalled entry engine.
- Supply was not the issue: 156 universe doublers in 2025 (5.7 %, above most years). Only ~48 reached top-20, and No_structural_stop skipped them like it skips 87–97 % of all top-N decisions every year. Gold miners (HL, AU, NGD, IAG) and memory names (WDC, STX) never reached top-20 in 2024–25.

**Why:** V-rebound miss is structural (Stage 4→2 needs bases; book-faithful). The extra 2025 cost is selection (top of funnel, cf. [[project_monster_funnel_top_of_funnel]]) plus no fat-tail winner (cf. [[project_edge_is_the_fat_tail]]).
**How to apply:** treat 2025 as a funnel/tail problem, not a gate or exposure one. See [[project_recovery_reentry_gap]].
