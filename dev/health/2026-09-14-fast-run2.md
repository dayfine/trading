## Method note — why the build gate is CI-derived this run

Step 6.1 prescribes a local `dune build && dune runtest`. This run deliberately
did **not** run it, and the substitution is recorded here rather than glossed.

Two reasons, both measured:

1. **It would have blocked the dispatched agents.** Per
   `.claude/rules/container-capacity-scheduling.md` this runner is memory-bound
   (15 GB) with a hard cap of **one `dune` in flight**. Three writing agents were
   in flight throughout this report's window, coordinating through a filesystem
   mutex (`/tmp/dune-mutex`). An orchestrator-held `dune` would have serialized
   all three behind a ~25-minute build.
2. **Run 1 today did exactly that and lost three agents' work.** Its entire turn
   was 886 s (step 8 of run 34853606164); the serial pre-dispatch gate consumed
   most of it, agents were dispatched at the end, and the container was torn down
   with all three in flight — zero branches, zero PRs. Filed as #2810, recurrence
   of #2747.

CI publishes the same measurement for the same commit, for free, across a **wider**
surface than a local `dune runtest` in this container (it runs the full linter
suite). The checks below are the ones that need no dune and therefore no mutex, so
they were run directly and their exit codes read unpiped.
