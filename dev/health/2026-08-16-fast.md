that was a decision rather than an omission:

- Three QC agents were building concurrently on this 4-core / 15 GB / 64 GB-disk

  (`test_csv_snapshot_builder_cleanup`, a SIGTERM timing race) whose confirmed
  correlate is exactly "2-3 concurrent cold builds on this box". Adding a fourth
  build raises the odds of a spurious red, not the confidence in a green.
- GitHub's `build-and-test` on main's **exact tip** is the same gate run on an
  uncontended box, and it is green. That is stronger evidence, not weaker.
