## A trap worth recording for whoever reads a `runtest` log next

`dune runtest` output contains **two `FAIL:` strings that are not failures.**
They sit inside `OK:` lines emitted by the `sete_diagnostics_check` meta-linter,
which asserts *about* `FAIL:` lines and therefore quotes them. A
`grep -c 'FAIL:'` over the log returns 2 on a perfectly green tree.

`grep -c '^FAIL:'` (anchored) returns 0 and is the correct form — but the
robust check remains **the exit code**, read directly and never through a pipe.
Both QC agents independently hit and correctly diagnosed this today.
