first time this run's dispatcher has observed it directly, and the cause was a

at-cap agent wave, i.e. exactly the "check before launching anything long" rule.

Not retried under load, and deliberately **not** escalated as a repo defect —
it is an operator error against a known, documented constraint. Recorded so the
