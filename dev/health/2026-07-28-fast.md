Deliberate substitution, same call run 7 made, for two independent reasons:

1. **It is strictly stronger evidence.** The `build-and-test` job on `e34474e5`
   runs the full linter suite (`nesting_linter`, `linter_magic_numbers`,
   `fn_length_linter`, `file_length_linter`, `linter_mli_coverage`,
   `no_python_check`, `status_file_integrity_linter`) in a clean container. A
   local `dune runtest` in this container reproduces a subset of that.
2. **A local re-run here would have been actively misleading.** Three agents
   were dispatched and building concurrently on a 4-CPU / 16 GB box. Run 7's
   local gate was OOM-killed (exit **137**) under exactly this load, and a
   naive reading of 137 reports "main is RED" when nothing is wrong with main.
   Running a fourth concurrent OCaml build to produce a weaker signal, at real
   risk of manufacturing a false `[critical]`, is not a good trade.

The two checks that *are* cheap and deterministic (`status_file_integrity.sh`,
`index_size_linter.sh`) were run directly in this container and both exited 0.
