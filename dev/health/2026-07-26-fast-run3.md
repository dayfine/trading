  **re-raises at line 35** after exhausting all 6 attempts.
- #2009 carried **qc-structural APPROVED + qc-behavioral APPROVED** (both

- Runs 1 and 2 both reported `dune runtest` exit **0** in this same
  devcontainer image (run 2 on `96c4c5ff`, which already contained #2009).
  Combined with the intermittency reported since 07-19, that points to
  **host/CPU dependence**, not a clean regression.

### Environment (supporting the host-dependence reading)

- Runner CPU: `Intel(R) Xeon(R) 6973P-C`, AVX-512 present (`avx512f`,
  `avx512dq`, `avq512ifma`). OpenBLAS/LAPACK kernel dispatch is CPU-feature
  dependent, and marginal Cholesky positive-definiteness is exactly the kind
  of result that flips across kernels.
- The repo's own `ci.yml` has a dedicated **"CPU-flag + ta-lib smoke test"**
  step, i.e. CPU sensitivity is already a known concern here.
- **Different images:** CI runs `ghcr.io/dayfine/trading-ci:latest`; the
  orchestrator runs `ghcr.io/dayfine/trading-devcontainer:latest`.

### Why CI does not catch it

CI's `build-and-test` was **green** on `96e72c12` and on both of PR #2097's
tips. But `ci.yml` has a **`Cache _build`** step, and the tuner sources are
unchanged, so a restored cache lets `dune runtest` skip re-running
`test_bayesian_opt` entirely. **CI green is therefore consistent with either
"it passes in the CI image" or "the cached pass was reused".** From outside the
runner these cannot be distinguished — but it does mean CI may be structurally
unable to surface this, which is the exact cache-masking pathology
`.claude/rules/code-health-discipline.md` documents.

### Candidate mechanism (hypothesis, NOT established)

`chol_with_nugget_escalation` caps the added jitter at
`_chol_retry_max_relative_jitter (1e-2) *. signal_variance`. Since the RBF
kernel's diagonal *is* `signal_variance`, 5 escalations add ~5% of the
diagonal, which should normally restore positive-definiteness. So a cap that
is too small is the obvious suspect but does **not** obviously explain the
failure. Degenerate inputs — duplicate observation points, or `NaN`/`inf` in
the kernel matrix, against which no amount of jitter helps — fit the evidence
better. **I did not instrument it and root cause is unresolved.** Recorded as
a cleanup-backlog item rather than guessed at.
